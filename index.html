Weekly Training Split

Monday: Chest, Back, and Lower Abs

  • Smith machine incline barbell bench press
  • Seated machine press
  • Seated cable chest flies
  • Pull-ups
  • Chest-supported T-bar rows
  • Seated cable rows
  • Leg raises

Tuesday: Biceps, Triceps, Forearms, and Upper Abs

  • EZ bar preacher curls
  • Rope cable hammer curls
  • Overhead tricep rope cable extensions
  • Cable tricep pull-downs
  • Behind-the-back wrist curls
  • Reverse cable curls
  • Cable crunches

Wednesday: Legs

  • Hacklift squats (1 heavy set to failure)
  • Machine adduction (2 heavy sets to failure)
  • Romanian deadlifts (2 heavy sets to failure)
  • Lying hamstring curls (2 heavy sets to failure)
  • Donkey calf raises (3 heavy sets to failure)

Thursday: Pull Day (Back, Biceps, Rear Deltoids) and Lower Abs

  • 45 degrees pulldowns (unilateral)
  • Seated barbbell goodmornings
  • Chest-supported T-bar rows
  • EZ bar preacher curls
  • Rope cable hammer curls
  • Rear deltoid cable flies
  • Leg raises

Friday: Push Day (Chest, Triceps, Anterior and Lateral Deltoids) and Upper Abs

  • Smith machine incline barbell bench press
  • Seated chest cable flies
  • Overhead rope tricep extensions
  • Skullcrushers
  • Seated lateral raises
  • Dumbbell shoulder press
  • Cable crunches

Saturday: Deltoids, Forearms and Calves

  • Standing cable lateral raises
  • Rear deltoid cable flies
  • Reverse cable curls (thumbless grip)
  • Behind-the-back wrist curls
  • Donkey calf raises
  • Seated calf raises
