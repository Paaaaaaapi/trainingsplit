Weekly Training Split

Monday: Chest, Back, Shoulders, and Abs

  • Smith machine incline barbell bench press
  • Seated lateral raises
  • Seated cable chest flies
  • Lat pulldowns
  • Overhead rear delt flies
  • Chest-supported T-bar rows
  • Kneeling cable crunches

Tuesday: Biceps, Triceps, Forearms, and Abs

  • EZ bar preacher curls
  • Rope cable hammer curls
  • JM Press
  • Rope tricep pull-downs
  • Sulek Curls
  • Reverse EZ cable curls
  • Cable crunches

Wednesday: Legs

  • Hacklift squats/Seated Leg Press
  • Leg extensions
  • Stiff-legged deadlifts
  • Lying hamstring curls
  • Kettlebell tibialis raises
  • Standing leg raises
